Features

Lobbies

When crossplay is active, lobbies are EOS lobbies that Steam players, Epic players and Device ID players all share. Without EOS they are Steam lobbies. The nodes are the same.

Create​

Create Crossplay Lobby creates a lobby and makes the player its owner.

SettingDefaultDescription
Max Members41 to 64.
VisibilityPublicSee below.
BucketdefaultSearches only find lobbies in the same bucket. Use it for game mode, region or build version.
AttributesEmptyTyped lobby attributes. See Attributes.
VisibilityEOSSteam
PublicFound by searchesPublic lobby
Friends OnlyJoinable through presence, not found by searchesFriends only lobby

Find​

Find Crossplay Lobbies searches with a Crossplay Lobby Search:

FieldDefaultDescription
BucketdefaultThe bucket to search in.
FiltersEmptyTyped filters, for example skill >= 1000. See Search filters.
Max Results201 to 200.

Join and leave​

  • Join Crossplay Lobby joins by Lobby Id, from a search result, an invite or a join request. It recognizes Steam lobby ids, so a Steam invite can be joined even when new lobbies are created in EOS.
  • Leave Crossplay Lobby leaves the current lobby. When the owner leaves, EOS gives the lobby to another member.

A player is in one lobby at a time. Create and Join fail with Invalid Parameters ("Already in a lobby, leave it first") while the player is in a lobby. A full lobby gives Lobby Full.

The current lobby​

NodeDescription
Get Current LobbyThe lobby the player is in, with its members and attributes.
Is In LobbyWhether the player is in a lobby.
Is Lobby OwnerWhether the local player owns the lobby.
Get Lobby Attribute / Get Member AttributeRead one attribute.

A Crossplay Lobby has a Lobby Id, Owner, Max Members, Num Members, Visibility, Crossplay (true for EOS lobbies), Attributes, Members and Match Address (set once a match has started). Each member has a User Id, Display Name and Attributes.

Events​

Bind these on the Crossplay Subsystem:

EventWhen
On Lobby UpdatedThe lobby changed: attributes, members, owner or match address. Gives the whole lobby.
On Lobby Member JoinedA member joined. Fires once the member's display name has arrived, at most 5 seconds after the join.
On Lobby Member LeftA member left or was kicked.
On Lobby LeftThe player is no longer in the lobby: they left, were kicked or the lobby was closed.

A member who leaves before On Lobby Member Joined fired gives neither event.
