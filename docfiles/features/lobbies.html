Features

Lobbies

When crossplay is active, lobbies are EOS lobbies that Steam players, Epic players and Device ID players all share. Without EOS they are Steam lobbies. The nodes are the same.

Create​

CreateCrossplayLobby creates a lobby and makes the player its owner.

SettingDefaultDescription
Max Members41 to 64.
VisibilityPublicSee below.
BucketdefaultSearches only find lobbies in the same bucket. Use it for game mode, region or build version.
AttributesEmptyTyped lobby attributes. See Attributes.

VisibilityEOSSteam
PublicFound by searchesPublic lobby
Friends OnlyJoinable through presence, not found by searchesFriends only lobby
Invite OnlyOnly with an invitePrivate lobby

Find​

FindCrossplayLobbies searches with a Crossplay Lobby Search:

FieldDefaultDescription
BucketdefaultThe bucket to search in.
FiltersEmptyTyped filters, for example skill >= 1000. See Search filters.
Max Results201 to 200.

Join and leave​

  • JoinCrossplayLobby joins by Lobby Id, from a search result, an invite or a join request. It recognizes Steam lobby ids, so a Steam invite can be joined even when new lobbies are created in EOS.
  • LeaveCrossplayLobby leaves the current lobby. When the owner leaves, EOS gives the lobby to another member.

A player is in one lobby at a time. Create and Join fail with Invalid Parameters ("Already in a lobby, leave it first") while the player is in a lobby. A full lobby gives Lobby Full.

Read a lobby without joining​

GetCrossplayLobby reads a lobby by Lobby Id without joining it, for example to show an invite with the lobby's name, mode and number of players. It returns the lobby's settings and attributes. Members is only filled in for the lobby the player is in.

Manage the lobby​

Only the lobby owner can call these. Others get Not Owner.

NodeDescription
KickCrossplayLobbyMemberRemoves a member from the lobby. The owner can't kick themselves; leave the lobby instead.
PromoteCrossplayLobbyMemberMakes another member the owner.
UpdateCrossplayLobbySettingsChanges Max Members and Visibility. Max Members can't be lower than the number of members. Bucket and Voice Chat can't be changed after the lobby is created.

Kicked players​

The kicked player gets On Kicked From Lobby and then On Lobby Left. The others get On Lobby Member Left.

A kicked player can't join the lobby again until the owner sends them a new invite with SendCrossplayInvite.

  • EOS lobbies remove the member on the EOS side.
  • Steam lobbies have no kick. The owner writes the kicked player in the lobby data, and the kicked player's game leaves by itself. A Steam client without CrossplayCore doesn't do this.

Lobby chat​

SendCrossplayLobbyChatMessage sends a text message to everyone in the lobby. The members get On Lobby Chat Message with the Lobby Id, the sender as a Crossplay Lobby Member, and the message. Messages are at most 256 characters; longer ones are cut. Messages from blocked players are not passed on.

  • Steam lobbies use Steam's lobby chat.
  • EOS lobbies have no chat in EOS, so the message is sent as a member attribute. That is enough for lobby chat, but not for fast chat: EOS limits how often a lobby can be updated.

The current lobby​

NodeDescription
Get Current LobbyThe lobby the player is in, with its members and attributes.
Is In LobbyWhether the player is in a lobby.
Is Lobby OwnerWhether the local player owns the lobby.
Get Lobby Attribute / Get Member AttributeRead one attribute.

A Crossplay Lobby has a Lobby Id, Owner, Max Members, Num Members, Visibility, Crossplay (true for EOS lobbies), Attributes, Members and Match Address (set once a match has started). Each member has a User Id, Display Name and Attributes.

Events​

Bind these on the Crossplay Subsystem:

EventWhen
On Lobby UpdatedThe lobby changed: attributes, members, owner or match address. Gives the whole lobby.
On Lobby Member JoinedA member joined. Fires once the member's display name has arrived, at most 5 seconds after the join.
On Lobby Member LeftA member left or was kicked.
On Lobby LeftThe player is no longer in the lobby: they left, were kicked or the lobby was closed.
On Kicked From LobbyThe owner kicked the player. On Lobby Left follows.
On Lobby Chat MessageA member sent a chat message.

A member who leaves before On Lobby Member Joined fired gives neither event.
